Chat demo

Every state of the shared chat, with hard-coded messages. Nothing on this page is sent to the server.

Open chat

Send a message and it appears on top of the list, and the field is emptied. An empty message or one longer than 1000 characters shows the reason under the field.

Locked chat

The messages can be read, and a notice stands in place of the form.

Server error

Every send is rejected: the server's message stands under the form and the text stays.

Privileged viewer

A moderator sees Delete next to other people's messages, with a confirmation, and Mute next to authors who may be muted. Muted users are listed above the messages, each with Unmute.

Muted viewer

The messages can be read, and a notice stands in place of the form.
